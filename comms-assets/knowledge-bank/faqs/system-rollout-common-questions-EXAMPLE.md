## Questions that come up on nearly every system-rollout change, with answer shapes

**"What happens to the data/records already in the old system?"** Answer shape: state
whether it migrates automatically, needs manual action, or is retained read-only, and for
how long. Never leave this unanswered — it's usually the first practical worry once
"what's changing" is understood.

**"Do I need to do anything before the go-live date, or does it just happen?"** Answer
shape: a yes/no up front, then the specific action if yes. Burying this in paragraph three
is the most common reason a mandatory pre-go-live action gets missed.

**"What if something goes wrong on day one?"** Answer shape: name the support channel
explicitly (this is the "help" mandatory message, restated as a question) and the hours it
covers. "IT will help" without a channel name doesn't answer this.

**"Why wasn't I consulted / why now?"** Answer shape: this is the "why" message, but
audiences ask it more sharply when they feel the timing was imposed rather than explained.
Reuse the narrative's "why now" content rather than writing a separate, softer answer —
consistency matters here specifically because this question tests whether the "why" was
genuine.

**"Does this affect my [team/role/pay/reporting line]?"** Answer shape: this is exactly
what `narrative/change-narrative-frame-EXAMPLE.md`'s "what stays the same" section exists
to pre-empt. If the answer is genuinely "no," say so plainly rather than leaving it
implied.
