02

Name the audiences

A change rarely lands the same way on everyone. Add a row per group that needs something different — drafting then runs per audience, per channel, not one broadcast draft for all of them. Leave this empty to draft one version for everyone.

e.g. “All staff”, ~2,400, self-service portal and pay date change — and separately “Line managers”, ~120, also pick up timesheet approval.
