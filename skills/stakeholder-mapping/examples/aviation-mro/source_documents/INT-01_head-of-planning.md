# Interview notes: Head of Planning and Production Control

Interviewee: Ng Wei Lun, Head of Planning and Production Control. Interviewer: CM team. 14 Sep 2026.

- On how Planning works with Stores: "My planners ring Stores before they raise anything. If Stores say it's not on the shelf we plan around it."
- On the release process: "Engineers sign off at the end and they hold the release. When Raj says the card's not ready, the hangar waits, no matter what my schedule says."
- On night shift: "Night shift re-sequences the cards themselves. By the time the plan reaches them it's already wrong. I find out in the morning."
- On the programme: "I want the system. I don't want to be the team that loses a week of planning to it during cutover."
