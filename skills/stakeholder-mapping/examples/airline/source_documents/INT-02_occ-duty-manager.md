# Interview notes: Senior duty manager, Operations Control Centre

Fictional interview with a Meridian Air manager. Interviewer: CM team. 9 Sep 2026.

- "At 2am nobody reads a procedure. I phone the people I trust: the on-duty captain, the engineer on call, and Farid's night scheduler."
- "The system has to tell me in seconds what a delay does to crew legality. If it takes a minute I will go back to the whiteboard."
- "Engineering sign off the aircraft availability that we plan against. If the engineer on call says it's not ready, nothing I plan matters."
- Asserted about others: "The line pilots do what the Chief Pilot's office says about anything that changes their duty patterns."
