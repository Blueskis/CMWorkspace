## 2. FAQ (6 Q&As)

**Q: Do I need to learn a new approval process?**
No. Approval routing and approvers are unchanged — only the entry screen changes.

**Q: What happens to ME51N after 16 Nov?**
Access is removed on go-live day. Use the new Fiori app from then on.

**Q: Where do I find the new app?**
Search "Create Purchase Requisition - Advanced" in your Fiori launchpad. It will also
appear as a new tile in your default group from 16 Nov.

**Q: What if I have a PR in progress in ME51N when the switch happens?**
Complete and submit any in-progress PRs before 16 Nov. Anything not submitted will need
to be re-created in the new app.

**Q: Is training mandatory?**
Yes — the 15-minute e-learning must be completed before go-live to keep your requester
access active.

**Q: Who do I contact if I'm stuck?**
Procurement Shared Services helpdesk: [contact]. They can also arrange a 1:1 walkthrough.
