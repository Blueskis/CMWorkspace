Training Material Generator

Build a training deck from your documents

Upload your client's slide template and the source documents (an FSD or similar spec) — this page parses them, drafts a training deck against your template's own layouts, and hands you a first-draft .pptx with knowledge-check questions and a QA report, ready for practitioner review.

Your files never leave this browser — there is no upload, no server. Only the text extracted from your source documents is sent to Claude (with your permission, asked before the first request) to draft the slide content. Screenshots and the template itself are never transmitted.
First-draft training material for practitioner review — not finished content.
