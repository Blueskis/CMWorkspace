## Situation

A multinational manufacturer replacing three regional HR systems with a single platform
across 5,000 employees in eleven countries. Two prior attempts had stalled; local HR teams
regarded the programme as a headcount exercise.

## What we did

Ran the impact assessment at country level rather than regionally, which surfaced that
resistance was concentrated in two markets with genuinely different processes, not spread
evenly. Redirected the engagement budget accordingly — deep participative design in those
two markets, lighter-touch comms elsewhere. Built a change-agent network of 40 local
volunteers rather than appointing regional leads.

## Outcome

Adoption reached 87% of the target population within 90 days of go-live, against a
programme target of 75%. Support ticket volume peaked at half the level forecast from the
previous rollout. Both previously-resistant markets came in above the global average.

## Why it's relevant

Directly applicable to multi-country rollouts where a prior attempt has already failed —
the recovery pattern (re-baseline at local level, reallocate rather than increase spend) is
the transferable part.
