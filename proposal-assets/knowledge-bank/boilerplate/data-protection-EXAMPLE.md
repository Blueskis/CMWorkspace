## Handling of client data

All client information is processed under the firm's information security policy and the
data processing terms agreed at contract. Personal data encountered during change activity
— stakeholder lists, training records, survey responses — is processed only for the
purposes of the engagement, held for the contracted retention period, and returned or
destroyed at close.

## Practical controls during the engagement

Stakeholder and impact data is held in the client's own environment where the client
provides one. Where it isn't, it's held in a segregated workspace with access limited to
the named engagement team.

Survey and feedback data is reported in aggregate. Free-text responses are anonymised
before they reach any client-facing artifact, since attributable feedback suppresses
response rates on exactly the questions that matter most.
