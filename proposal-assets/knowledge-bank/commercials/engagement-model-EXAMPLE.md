## Engagement structure

Phased with a commercial break at each phase gate, so the client can stop, resize, or
extend at a known point. Assess is typically fixed-price on a defined deliverable set;
Build and Conduct are capped time-and-materials against an agreed resourcing profile.

## Standard assumptions

- Client provides a named business sponsor and business-unit change champions, with
  time formally allocated rather than absorbed alongside a full role.
- Client provides access to affected staff for the impact assessment and training
  delivery within the agreed windows.
- Training delivery assumes client-provided facilities and system environments.
- Travel and expenses charged at cost against an agreed cap, invoiced monthly in arrears.

## Standard exclusions

Technical configuration, data migration, systems integration testing, and content
translation, unless explicitly scoped and priced separately.

## Rate card

Held separately and confirmed per bid — rate cards change, and a rate copied from a
previous submission is the most common source of a commercially wrong bid.
