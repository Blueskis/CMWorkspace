## Change Lead

Owns the change approach and the relationship with the programme SRO. Accountable for the
impact assessment, the engagement plan, and readiness sign-off at each phase gate.
Typically 0.8–1.0 FTE across the engagement.

## Engagement & Communications Manager

Runs the stakeholder engagement plan, the change-agent network, and all programme
communications. Owns the comms calendar and the pulse survey instrument.

## Training Lead

Owns the training needs analysis, curriculum design, materials, and delivery scheduling.
Ramps up through Build and peaks either side of go-live.

## Business Readiness Analyst

Maintains the impact assessment at process level, tracks readiness by business unit, and
produces the weekly status pack. Usually the role that catches a unit falling behind
before the unit reports it.

## Shaping principle

Roles are staffed to phase rather than held flat across the engagement — the training and
readiness roles are near-zero during Assess and peak during Conduct. A flat resourcing
profile across a rollout is usually a sign the plan hasn't been thought through.
