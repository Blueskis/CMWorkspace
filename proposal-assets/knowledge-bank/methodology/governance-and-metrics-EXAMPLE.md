## Reporting cadence

A weekly change status pack into the programme board, a fortnightly readiness review with
business owners, and a phase-gate review at each transition. The phase gate is the only
one with a decision attached — the others are for visibility, and treating them as
approval points slows delivery without improving it.

## Adoption metrics

Named measures rather than "KPIs to be agreed":

- **Readiness** — proportion of the affected population who have completed role-based
  training, by business unit.
- **Adoption** — proportion of transactions completed in the new system versus workarounds,
  measured weekly from go-live.
- **Proficiency** — support-ticket volume per 100 users, trended; a flat or rising curve
  four weeks post-go-live indicates a training gap, not a system defect.
- **Sentiment** — a three-question pulse at baseline, go-live, and 90 days.

## Escalation

Change risks route through the programme risk register rather than a parallel change log.
A separate register is where change risks go to be ignored — the programme board sees one
list or it prioritises the technical one.

## Delivery risk review

A structured delivery-risk score at each phase gate, scoring review cadence, team
integrity, sponsor commitment, local commitment, and incremental effort. The score's value
is the trend across gates, not the absolute number at any one.
