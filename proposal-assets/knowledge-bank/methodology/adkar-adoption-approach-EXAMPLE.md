## Approach

A four-phase structure that runs alongside the technical delivery plan rather than after
it, so change activity lands before the system does.

**Assess.** Establish the change baseline — who is affected, how far their day-to-day work
moves, and where the resistance will concentrate. Outputs: change impact assessment,
stakeholder map, readiness baseline.

**Build.** Construct the case for change and the engagement infrastructure. Outputs:
change narrative, comms plan, change-agent network, training needs analysis.

**Conduct.** Deliver the engagement, training, and support through go-live. Outputs:
training delivery, floorwalking and hypercare, adoption dashboard.

**Sustain.** Transfer ownership to the business and confirm the change held. Outputs:
adoption review, reinforcement plan, handover to BAU owners.

## Why this shape

The phases are sequenced so each one's output is the next one's input — the impact
assessment scopes the training needs analysis, which scopes delivery. Phases can compress
under a tight timeline, but skipping Assess to save time is what produces the classic
failure of a well-run rollout that nobody adopts.

## Diagnostic instrumentation

Each phase has a diagnostic checkpoint drawn from the firm's framework toolkit — a
delivery-risk score at phase gates, a technical-vs-adaptive read during Assess to size the
engagement, and a stakeholder-network read during Sustain to confirm ownership has
genuinely transferred.
